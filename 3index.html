01 / ABOUT

Researching the intelligence behind construction cost.

Dr. Oluwafunmibi Seun Idowu is a Quantity Surveyor and construction-management researcher whose documented work focuses on improving cost and quantity information at the early stages of construction projects.

His doctoral research at City University of Hong Kong examined conceptual quantity models for building envelopes and structural elements, with an emphasis on quantitative and data-driven approaches.

His professional background spans quantity surveying, construction planning and the wider built environment, combining professional practice with research into improved approaches to construction cost and quantity estimation.

“
Solving complex problems through the principle of “Simplicity” is a philosophical stance he embraces.

The principle seeks to reduce complexity to its essential elements, clarify the problem, and develop solutions that are practical, efficient and intelligible without compromising quality or rigour.
