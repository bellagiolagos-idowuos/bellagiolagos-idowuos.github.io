Dr. O. S. Idowu Academic & Professional Profile

01 / ABOUT

Researching the intelligence behind construction cost.

Dr. Oluwafunmibi Seun Idowu is a Quantity Surveyor and construction-management researcher whose documented work focuses on improving cost and quantity information at the early stages of construction projects.

His doctoral research at City University of Hong Kong examined conceptual quantity models for building envelopes and structural elements, with an emphasis on quantitative and data-driven approaches.

His professional background also spans quantity surveying, construction planning and the wider built environment.

02 / RESEARCH

Research interests

01

Conceptual Quantity Estimation

03

Modelling relationships between building-envelope characteristics, quantities and cost.

Statistical and machine-learning methods applied to construction planning and management.

05 / PROFESSIONAL

Quantity surveying & the built environment

Dr. Oluwafunmibi Seun Idowu is an active member of the Nigerian Institute of Quantity Surveyors (NIQS) and the Quantity Surveyors Registration Board of Nigeria (QSRBN), which reflects his dedication to professional development and adherence to industry standards.

An accomplished and results-oriented quantity surveyor with over 25 years of industry experience in the built environment. Have been involved in over 30 capital projects (private and public) in the last 20 years in Nigeria as a Project Manager, Contract Manager, Lead Planner and Quantity Surveyor on various projects.

The New Federal High Court, Ikoyi in Lagos; Chevron Food storage building and Mess Hall, Escravos in Warri; Maritime Academy Jetty, Oron in Akwa-Ibom, are some notable past projects undertaken. Currently oversees various projects mid-rise and high-rise projects with a total value of approximately forty billion Naira for the Cavalli Business and Investment Group.

Solving complex problems by adopting the principle of ‘Simplicity’ is a philosophical stance he embraces—one that seeks to reduce complexity to its essential elements, clarify the problem, and develop solutions that are practical, efficient, and intelligible without compromising quality or rigour
