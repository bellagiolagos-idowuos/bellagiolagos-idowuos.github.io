About

I am a researcher specializing in construction cost engineering, machine learning applications in the built environment, and digital construction technologies. My work focuses on developing intelligent models for early-stage cost estimation, conceptual quantity prediction, and web-based decision support tools for construction professionals.

I completed my doctoral research at City University of Hong Kong, where I developed novel frameworks for predicting building envelope quantities and costs using advanced computational methods without reliance on preliminary sketch designs [[1]].
