About Me

I am a researcher specializing in construction cost engineering and digital technologies. My work bridges the gap between traditional construction management and advanced computational methods like Machine Learning and Support Vector Machines.

I completed my doctoral research at City University of Hong Kong, developing novel frameworks for predicting building envelope quantities without reliance on preliminary sketch designs.
