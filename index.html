Biography

Dr. Idowu is a researcher focused on the application of machine learning and web-based tools in construction cost engineering. His work aims to improve the accuracy of early-stage cost planning through empirical modeling and support vector regression.

He holds a Ph.D. from City University of Hong Kong, where his thesis explored conceptual quantity models for building envelopes.
